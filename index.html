What is Data and Information Management?

Data and information management refer to how data will be gathered, verified and validated to ensure the information is in the proper format and complete. With context the data becomes information that can be used to take action , but this information will need to be properly stored, kept safe and maintained throughout its lifecycle.

The farmer will fill out the required forms (harvest yield , spoilage , crop type etc) on an app, and the form response will be saved locally on the phone. The response will then be checked if it is correct and complete to ensure data accuracy . Once the application has determined that there is a stable network connection the form response will be sent to the central cloud database and will be synced with the batch data.

This allows the farmers to discard manual paperwork, while being able to maintain the convenience of software forms, and the offline caching solves the reality that not every region in South Africa has proper internet connectivity .
