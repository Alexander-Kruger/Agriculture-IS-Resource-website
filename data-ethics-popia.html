1. Data Ethics, Privacy, and POPIA Compliance

Active Mandate

To ensure we strictly follow the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the integrated platforms will have to actively secure the following:

badge

Farmer identity details, such as their names, contact numbers and ID numbers, as well as their financial profile details like their bank account number, which is required to securely send and receive funds.

pin_drop

Precise geographical coordinates and physical addresses of where each farmer’s field can be located.

inventory_2

The specific crop outputs, linked to the farm they came from, e.g. Farm A- 1500Kg or Farm B- 900Kg.

sensors

Tracking Logs for kilograms spoiled by transit per hour as well as device identifiers transmitting the Mobile network status.

3. How Data Flows: Old vs. New

close

Old System

In the old system, data is not safely shared between users. Farmers write down their crop weights on paper logs or spreadsheets. When these unprotected files are shared through basic messages or emails, anyone can see the users private bank details and exact GPS maps. This allows big corporate retailers to see individual farm capacities, which lets them manipulate market prices. It also creates a massive risk for data leaks which break privacy laws.

warning Unprotected logs & spreadsheet leaks expose GPS maps and bank numbers to retailers.
check

New Cloud ERP System

The new cloud ERP system fixes this problem by using a secure solution. When a worker types raw field inputs into the app, an automated privacy tool instantly removes all personal names and exact locations. The data is then sent to a secure dashboard that limits what people can see based on their jobs. This means managers get the broad numbers they need to plan logistics, while the users private information remains totally protected.

5. Limitation (The Main Weakness)

The biggest weakness of our application will be human mistakes.

If a manager downloads an open Excel list of the R12.6m of Revenue onto a personal phone, or if workers share passwords, the digital locks fail.

school

The system is only safe if staff receive regular training on how to handle private data.
